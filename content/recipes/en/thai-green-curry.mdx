A weeknight-friendly green curry that leans on a good-quality paste and fresh
thai basil. Adjust the chilli to taste, and don't skip the step of frying the
paste — it's where the depth comes from.

> Tip: swap the chicken for firm tofu and use a vegan paste to make it plant-based.
